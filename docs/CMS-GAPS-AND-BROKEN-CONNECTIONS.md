## Counts and severity boundaries

- **9 disconnected business field definitions:** F01 (1), F02 (3), F04 (4), F05 (1).
- **10 defective/misleading active admin controls:** those nine fields plus F06’s Move later action. An action is not another public content field in the mapping denominator.
- **6 partially connected business fields:** four duplicate navigation slot fields, desktop focal position, and zero-price teaser output. The zero-price case is a presentation condition, not a tenth disconnected editor field.
- **1 unnecessary duplicated configuration family:** navigation. Catalog SEO is disconnected overlap, not a second active public SEO authority. Old `place.*`, retired intro/Tour keys, and historical home slots are compatibility data, not active duplicate editors.
- **0 confirmed P0**, **2 P1 finding groups** (F01/F04). P2/P3 items above are not authorization to implement.

## Not defects / intentionally constrained

Empty Partners hides the section until approved active logos exist. Legal pages can return a publication notice while their text/status is unpublished; HTTP 200 does not establish approved legal content. Stale/unavailable live tracking is honestly reported; upstream GPS accuracy was not certified. `/live` redirects to `/route`; `/tour` is a 301 redirect, not a missing CMS page. English-only EN is an indicator, not a translation selector. Pricing, capacity allocation, authentication, GPS, ETA, immutable confirmations, CSS and licensed asset provenance should stay protected. Editorial destination names/photos and operational map labels/order are intentionally distinct owners; association defines their shared presentation.
